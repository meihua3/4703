Our Mission

Every public digital announcement must now pass the Digital Fairness Audit before release. This process ensures that each message is accessible, understandable, and inclusive—across all official languages.

The audit may slow down publishing, but it guarantees that no citizen is left behind.

About This System

This system evolved from the 2025 Multilingual Announcement Feed project, which revealed that non-native speakers struggled to access verified information. The FairCity Check portal institutionalises fairness evaluation to ensure that every announcement is readable by all citizens, even if it takes longer to publish.
